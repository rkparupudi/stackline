    Numbers worth memorising

    Rounded orders of magnitude for quick estimates. Real latencies vary a lot with hardware, region and load.

    FactRounded valueWhy it helps
    Seconds in a day86,400 ≈ 10⁵Converts “per day” into “per second” instantly.
    1 million requests per day≈ 12 per secondScale up: 100 M/day ≈ 1,200/s.
    1 KB × 1 million1 GBStorage per day from writes × size.
    Main memory read~100 nanosecondsWhy caches are so fast.
    SSD random read~tens to hundreds of microsecondsRoughly 1,000× slower than memory.
    Round trip inside one data centre~0.5 millisecondsEach extra service hop adds up.
    Round trip across continents~100–150 millisecondsWhy CDNs and regional deployments exist.

    Live labs: break it yourself

    These are real simulations running in your browser. Change the knobs and watch the numbers. You'll remember a failure you caused far longer than one you read about.

    Load balancer

    Requests stream in continuously. Server C is twice as slow as the others. Switch algorithms, or take servers down, and watch where the work piles up.

    Consistent hashing

    24 keys on a hash ring. Add or remove a node and compare how many keys must move with consistent hashing and with plain hash % N.

    Token-bucket rate limiter

    Each request spends one token. Tokens refill at a steady rate, up to the bucket's capacity. Try a burst.
